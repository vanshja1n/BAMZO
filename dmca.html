DMCA Copyright Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

BAMZO respects intellectual-property rights. Some games and related media accessible through the directory are hosted by third parties and remain the property of their respective rights holders.

Copyright removal notices

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe material accessible through this website infringes your rights, you may submit a request for review. A useful notice should contain:

  • Your name and a reliable way to contact you.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed.
  • The exact URL on BAMZO where the material appears or is linked.
  • Enough information for us to identify the material at issue.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

How to submit a notice

Use the method described on our Contact page and clearly label the request “Copyright Notice.” Because the listed issue tracker is public, do not publish identity documents, private addresses or other sensitive information there. If sensitive information is necessary, state that you need a private contact method without posting the sensitive material itself.

Review and removal

We review sufficiently detailed notices in good faith. Depending on the circumstances, we may correct a link, disable access to material or request additional information needed to identify the work and the complained-of material.

Third-party hosts

When the disputed material is hosted by an independent game provider rather than on this website, the rights holder may also need to contact that provider directly. We cannot remove files from servers we do not control.

Misrepresentation

False or materially misleading copyright claims can have legal consequences. If you are uncertain whether a use is authorized or legally permitted, consider obtaining qualified legal advice before submitting a notice.
