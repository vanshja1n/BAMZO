About BAMZO

BAMZO is an independent browser-game directory created and maintained by Vansh Jain. It helps visitors discover and play free browser games across categories including action, adventure, strategy, puzzle, casual, driving and sports.

We organize games into an easy-to-browse catalog with descriptions, controls and useful gameplay information.
